# Sunfield Mini-Mart

Unlock from the shop building, the Palengke's **My shop** button, or the Bagsakan business sheet. Requires level 4 and ₱6,000. The price includes a dedicated shopkeeper, Ate Lorna, and ₱180 of prepaid salary.

Stock is separate from the farm's bodega. Select goods and a free delivery vehicle, then choose the farmer or an existing hired driver through the normal driver picker. Goods are removed from the bodega when dispatched and added to shelves only when the vehicle arrives. Cancelling before departure refunds the reserved goods. Rice can come from purchased bigas or two palay per pack of bigas.

Customers arrive on foot from the market or residences. Existing road traffic can also park, walk into the shop, buy, and leave. Each completed visit can make one purchase of up to three units. Sales remove real shelf stock and immediately add pesos to the player's balance. Empty shelves and a closed or unpaid shop cannot generate sales.

The shop opens from 6 AM to 10 PM in game time. Salary is ₱60 per game day of opening time, drawn from a prepaid wallet. There is no debt: if prepaid salary runs out, the shop pauses until the player tops it up. Salary, sales, and customers do not simulate while the game is hidden or closed. A limited customer pool and existing cars keep the feature light on mobile devices.

Capacity is 180 units overall and 60 per product, including incoming deliveries. The shop sheet shows shelf stock, farm stock, incoming stock, low-stock warnings, delivery status, revenue, and wages. Sales totals are revenue, not profit; stock acquisition and salary are separate costs.
