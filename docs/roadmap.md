# Sunfield Farm — roadmap for the next update

From the request list of Nov 8, 2026. Each stage is built, play-tested in the browser, shown with screenshots, and approved before the next one starts.

## Done (branch `life-update`, not deployed yet)

- Android "notebook lines" across the screen: fixed (32-bit depth on WebGL2, a noise floor elsewhere).
- The SUNFIELD FARM arch now stands on the farm road where it crosses the fence, and fences never sit on a road.
- The big hint label is small now and fades after 4 seconds.
- Coins show as a peso coin, and every price reads ₱.

## Stages

| # | Stage | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Game-style controls** | Sketch-style game buttons everywhere. Tapping a building opens a small pop-up with three buttons (Collect · Upgrade · Info); each opens its own attached panel that folds away. The garage and upgrades use the same tidy layout. When you send a vehicle, you choose the driver: you or a hired driver, with busy people greyed out. More helpers can be hired. |
| B | **Sound** | Tap, collect, coin, plant, harvest, animal and vehicle sounds; a gentle background music loop. Separate on/off switches for effects and music in Settings. Generated in the game, so no audio files to license. |
| C | **Living animals** | Harvesting takes the live animals out of the house (eggs still come from layers). You restock by buying young (chicks, piglets, calves, goat kids, ducklings, tilapia fingerlings) at the poultry and agrivet store. They grow over a countdown of game days while you feed them. A new incubator building hatches eggs into chicks. Seasonal events: bird flu news in certain months, and heat waves that need extra water. Warnings show on the animal houses; animals can die if ignored. |
| D | **Katayan and livestock trucks** | A katayan (slaughterhouse) on your own farm road turns live animals into meat. Harvesting means driving a livestock truck to the area gate, reversing in, and loading; the animals move about in the truck's cages. A cheaper chicken truck replaces the sedan, plus a pig and cattle truck and a garage bay for them. The closed van stays the expensive city earner. Palengke and customers buy **live** or **meat** at different prices; the bagsakan takes meat only. Deliveries also go to city houses, mountain houses and small home businesses. |
| E | **Farmer's needs and fishing** | Crops need watering before they grow. Your farmer gets hungry and tired; rest at home, or cook. Catch tilapia with a fishing rod at your pond: a tap-timing minigame with an on-screen button. Cook the catch to eat. |
| F | **Vehicles** | Realistic doors that swing open and shut on every vehicle. Factory closed vans with a Filipino meat-brand livery. |
| G | **Transport and town life** | A provincial bus line with a bus terminal and a stop up in the mountain barangay, with people living and walking there. A jeepney terminal with a queue of idling jeepneys taking riders to the palengke and the factory gate. A police station with patrols day and night. Fiesta days with a parade in the barangay. |
| H | **Bigger map and the bay** | A larger world with the sea in one corner and a port. An unlockable bay business ships products for extra income. The camera can pan out to the mountains and the mountain barangay. |
| I | **Better cows and pigs** | Rounder, more realistic cattle and pig models, with smoother walking. |
